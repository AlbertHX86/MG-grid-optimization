Generated by `hosting_capacity/run_hosting.py` (Python 3.13.9, pandas 3.1.1, numpy 2.3.5, scipy 1.16.3). Do not edit by hand. Question and plan: issue #3.

↑ = higher is better, ↓ = lower is better; columns without an arrow are sizes. α = largest scaling of the portfolio injection pattern that meets every N-0 and N-1 limit (α ≥ 1 means the portfolio is deliverable as sited). Deliverable MW = min(α × need, energy headroom).

## Setup

- Network: EPE 2035 PWF, MG-centred equivalent (1,489 buses, 1,890 branches). Incremental DC flows anchored on the AC P flows; LODF for N-1 (verified against explicit outage re-solves, `check_engine.py`).
- Monitored: 555 branches = 283 lines (the original 283-line set) + 272 network-transformer branches (windings ≥138 kV, one ≥230 kV; generator step-ups excluded). Limits: normal rating (N-0), max(normal, emergency) (N-1).
- Contingencies: 437 (monitored lines, two-winding transformers, and the HV leg of each three-winding unit); 44 radial outages skipped.
- Base case without new resources: 0 N-0 and 1 N-1 violations: SGPARA-MG138-SGPAAC-MG000 +42.9 MW for loss of SGPARA-MG500-SGPAAB-MG000. Pre-existing violations only count against the new resources when worsened with a distribution factor ≥ 5%.
- Absorption cases (the old load-proportional convention is retired): **A1** MG hydro + thermal back-down (UHE/PCH/UTE, proportional to output), headroom 5,636 MW; **A2** Displace boundary inflows (BA/GO equivalents + Estreito HVDC infeed), headroom 23,616 MW; **B** Export to the rest of the SIN at current outflow boundaries (SP/RJ/ES), network-limited only. `+load2050` variants let 2050 MG load growth (2,236 MW at PWF scale, from the LP's 80.4 → 97.2 TWh) absorb injection first.
- Siting: nearest eligible station at the allocation weights (`reference_siting_YYYY.csv`); MILP sitings and BESS-at-load are sensitivities. Study conditions: mean LP output over the top 1% of hours.
- Provisional minor/major thresholds (issue #3). Two line rules are reported side by side: the **50 km rule** (the first implementation: a parallel circuit ≤ 50 km is minor, longer is major) and the **uprate rule**, which additionally treats a line needing ≤ +25% of its limit as an uprate/reconductor (minor) regardless of length. The uprate rule was added after the first results were seen and it changes several 2050 verdicts from major to minor, so read both columns. Adding transformer banks at an existing substation is minor; more than 10 upgrade groups is major; a case whose need exceeds its energy headroom is energy-limited.
