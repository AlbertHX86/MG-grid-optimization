## Setup

- Network: EPE 2035 PWF, MG-centred equivalent (1,489 buses, 1,890 branches). Incremental DC flows anchored on the AC P flows; LODF for N-1 (`check_engine.py`, `check_pypsa.py`). Scope: main grid only — 555 monitored branches = 283 lines ≥230 kV + 272 network-transformer branches; 437 contingencies (44 radial outages skipped). Limits: normal rating (N-0), max(normal, emergency) (N-1).
- Base case without new resources: 0 N-0 and 1 N-1 violations: SGPARA-MG138-SGPAAC-MG000 +42.9 MW for loss of SGPARA-MG500-SGPAAB-MG000.
- Absorption, main case (CAISO convention): **Pro rata: all other online generation scaled down (MG hydro/thermal + boundary inflows)**, headroom 29,253 MW, with the load at the target-year level (2050: +2,236 MW at PWF scale, pro rata to existing MG load). Sensitivities: A1 MG hydro + thermal back-down (UHE/PCH/UTE, proportional to output) (5,636 MW); A2 Displace boundary inflows (BA/GO equivalents + Estreito HVDC infeed) (23,616 MW); B Export to the rest of the SIN at current outflow boundaries (SP/RJ/ES) (network-limited only; it scales every existing outflow tie in proportion).
- Attribution: CAISO 5% circle — a facility row is tested only if a study unit (one per sited technology and area; distributed PV as a load-weighted aggregate) has DFAX ≥ 5% on it or a flow impact at study output ≥ 5% of its rating. Pre-existing overloads inside a circle may not get worse. Sensitivity: at most 20 units and 1,500 MW raised per facility (others at zero, a bound).
- Study conditions: off-peak deliverability = LP midday hours (S1, S2; top 1% of hours); peak deliverability = LP evening hours (S3) and the CAISO HSN/SSN definitions (exceedance values over all days).

## Constraint subset for the production-cost run

Rows (element, contingency) loaded ≥ 90% of their limit at full study output in any of the 220 circle-mode cases: 8,393 of 242,708 rows (3.5%), 66 elements in 30 groups. In-sample: α identical in 106 of 106 cases with α < 1 and the same deliverable/not verdict in 220 of 220 cases. Out of sample (subset from the reference siting only, 6,729 rows, tested on the other 160 cases): α identical in 71 of 71 cases with α < 1, same verdict in 160 of 160. Any solution built on the subset is re-checked on all rows.

## Not covered here

- **No midday base case.** The PWF is an evening snapshot (existing PV at 0 MW). The main tables add only the NEW resources to it; midday conclusions are conditional on the base case (see the existing-PV sensitivity).
- 138 kV and below are out of scope (main grid only). AC voltage, stability, N-2/P7 and the annual curtailment criterion are evaluated in later phases. The 2050 topology is the frozen 2035 PWF; one LP weather year.
- Upgrades relax ratings only; real reinforcements change impedances.
